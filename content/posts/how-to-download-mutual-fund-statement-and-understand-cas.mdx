## What Is a Mutual Fund Statement?

A mutual fund statement records information about an investor's holdings or transactions. Depending on the statement type, it may include scheme names, folio details, units held, transaction history, and valuation information.

A Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) provides a combined view of eligible mutual fund holdings and, in relevant cases, securities held in demat accounts.

## Where Can You Get a Mutual Fund Statement?

Investors can check the official portal of the relevant mutual fund, registrar, or recognised consolidated statement service.

In India, AMFI lists resources for requesting a consolidated account statement through CAMS and KFintech. Investors can also use the MFCentral portal to access relevant portfolio and CAS services.

Official resources:

- AMFI CAS information: https://www.amfiindia.com/online-center/download-cas
- CAMS consolidated statement: https://www.camsonline.com/Investors/Statements/Consolidated-Account-Statement
- KFintech consolidated statement: https://mfs.kfintech.com/investor/General/ConsolidatedAccountStatement
- MFCentral: https://www.mfcentral.com/

The available statements and steps can depend on your holdings and the information registered with the provider.

## How to Review Your Statement

After obtaining your statement, review the period covered, folio or account details, transaction entries, units, and displayed values.

If something appears incorrect, contact the relevant mutual fund or registrar through its official support channels. Keep sensitive information secure and avoid uploading full financial statements to untrusted websites.

A statement is a record of holdings or transactions. It is not the same as a mutual fund return calculator, which estimates a potential future value using assumptions.

## Conclusion

An official mutual fund statement helps you review recorded holdings and transactions. Use an official provider to request your statement, check the document carefully, and contact the provider directly if you spot a discrepancy.
