## What Is EDD and How Is It Defined?

In obstetrics, **EDD** stands for **Estimated Due Date**, also referred to as the estimated date of delivery. It represents the estimated calendar date on which spontaneous labour is most likely to occur.

A human pregnancy averages:
- **280 days** (40 completed weeks) from the first day of the Last Menstrual Period (LMP).
- **266 days** (38 completed weeks) from the time of conception and fertilization.

Because only **4% to 5% of babies** are delivered on their exact calculated due date, the EDD serves primarily as a vital clinical benchmark rather than a fixed deadline. Over **80% of healthy pregnancies** culminate safely between **37 weeks 0 days and 41 weeks 6 days** (the full-term window).

---

## How to Calculate EDD: Two Core Dating Methods

Clinicians rely on two primary clinical frameworks to establish the official due date:

### 1. EDD by LMP (Naegele's Rule)

The LMP method dates pregnancy from the **first day of your last normal menstrual period**, not the day bleeding stopped.

#### The Mathematical Formula:
$$\text{EDD} = \text{First Day of LMP} + 280\text{ days}$$

Or through the classic manual calendar shortcut:
1. Take the first day of your LMP.
2. Add 1 year.
3. Subtract 3 calendar months.
4. Add 7 days.

#### Adjusting for Cycle Length:
The classical rule assumes an idealized **28-day cycle** with ovulation occurring precisely on Day 14. If your regular cycle is longer or shorter, obstetric guidelines adjust the date accordingly:
$$\text{Adjusted EDD} = \text{LMP} + 280\text{ days} + (\text{Cycle Length} - 28\text{ days})$$

- **32-Day Cycle:** Add 4 additional days (ovulation occurred around Day 18).
- **26-Day Cycle:** Subtract 2 days (ovulation occurred around Day 12).

---

### 2. EDD by USG (Ultrasound Dating)

Ultrasound scans perform biometric measurements of the developing embryo or fetus:
- **First Trimester (Weeks 6–13):** Crown-Rump Length (CRL) is measured. Early first-trimester scans have an accuracy margin of $\pm 5$ to $7$ days and are medically considered the gold standard benchmark when LMP is uncertain or irregular.
- **Second Trimester (Weeks 14–22):** Measurements include Biparietal Diameter (BPD), Head Circumference (HC), Abdominal Circumference (AC), and Femur Length (FL).

#### Simplified Ultrasound Due Date Formula:
$$\text{Estimated EDD} = \text{Scan Date} + (280\text{ days} - \text{Gestational Age in Days at Scan})$$

For example, if an ultrasound performed on **April 1** records a gestational age of **12 weeks and 3 days** (87 days), the remaining days until 280 days is $280 - 87 = 193\text{ days}$. Adding 193 days to April 1 yields an EDD of **October 11**.

## Gestational Age vs. Fetal Age

A common point of confusion during prenatal checkups is why gestational age differs from the time since conception:

| Metric | Measured From | Duration at Full Term | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gestational Age (Menstrual Age)** | First day of LMP | 40 Weeks (280 Days) | Used on all medical charts, lab forms, and doctor reports. |
| **Fetal Age (Conceptional Age)** | Fertilization / Ovulation | 38 Weeks (266 Days) | Approximately 2 weeks shorter than gestational age. |

Because most women cannot pinpoint the exact hour or day of fertilization, international medical societies (including ACOG and WHO) universally standardize dating to **gestational age**.

## Pregnancy Trimesters & Timeline Milestones

A healthy pregnancy is clinically divided into three successive trimesters:

1. **First Trimester (Week 1 through Week 13 + 6 Days):**
   - Embryonic organogenesis, neural tube development, and early cardiac activity.
   - Recommended tests: Dating / Viability Ultrasound (Weeks 6–9), Nuchal Translucency / NT Scan (Weeks 11–13), Non-Invasive Prenatal Testing (NIPT).
2. **Second Trimester (Week 14 through Week 27 + 6 Days):**
   - Rapid fetal somatic growth and development of auditory and motor functions.
   - Recommended tests: Fetal Anomaly Scan (Level II / TIFFA, Weeks 18–20), Glucose Tolerance Test (OGTT, Weeks 24–28).
3. **Third Trimester (Week 28 through Week 40+):**
   - Alveolar lung maturation, rapid subcutaneous fat accumulation, and cephalic positioning.
   - Milestone: Reaches early term at **Week 37** and full term at **Week 39–40**.

## When to Seek Clinician Advice

Online calculators provide an educational starting point. Always consult your obstetrician or gynaecologist if:
- Your menstrual cycles are irregular, prolonged, or absent.
- You conceived while using oral contraceptives or intrauterine devices.
- Your early ultrasound dating differs from your LMP date by more than 7 days.
- You experience severe pelvic pain, heavy vaginal bleeding, or unexpected fluid discharge.
