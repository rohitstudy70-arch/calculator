## What Is a SIP Calculator?

A SIP calculator is an online tool that estimates the potential future value of regular mutual fund investments. SIP stands for Systematic Investment Plan, a method of investing a fixed amount at regular intervals.

A SIP calculator typically uses the monthly contribution, investment period, and an assumed return rate to estimate the future investment value.

It can help you explore different savings scenarios, but it cannot predict actual market returns.

## How Does a SIP Calculator Work?

The calculator uses a future-value formula to estimate the value of periodic contributions.

For contributions made at the end of each month, one commonly used formula is:

**FV = P × [((1 + i)^n − 1) ÷ i]**

Where:

- FV is the estimated future value.
- P is the monthly SIP contribution.
- i is the assumed monthly rate of return.
- n is the number of monthly contributions.

If payments are assumed to occur at the beginning of each month, the calculation may include an additional factor of (1 + i). Results therefore depend on the calculation method and contribution timing.

## Example of a Monthly SIP

Suppose you plan to invest ₹5,000 per month for 10 years.

Your total contributions would be:

₹5,000 × 120 months = ₹6,00,000

A calculator can then apply a hypothetical return assumption to estimate a future value. The final projected amount depends on the assumed rate and formula. It is not a guarantee of future wealth.

## What Inputs Do You Need?

Most SIP calculators ask for the following information:

1. Monthly investment amount.
2. Investment duration.
3. Assumed annual rate of return.

Some tools may also offer a step-up SIP option, which increases the contribution at set intervals.

Before comparing two calculators, check whether they use the same return conversion, contribution timing, and frequency. Different calculation methods can produce different results.

## SIP Calculator vs Mutual Fund Return Calculator

A SIP calculator focuses on recurring contributions. A mutual fund return calculator may support SIP, lump sum, or both, depending on the tool.

Use a SIP calculator to estimate the value of regular payments. Use a lump sum calculator to estimate the potential future value of an initial investment.

## Are SIP Calculator Results Guaranteed?

No. Mutual fund returns are not guaranteed. The calculator applies a mathematical assumption to estimate a future value, while real investments change with market conditions and fund performance.

Use projections as educational illustrations, not as promises or personalised investment recommendations.

## Conclusion

A SIP calculator can help you understand how contribution amounts, duration, and assumed rates affect an estimated investment value. Compare multiple scenarios and verify the calculator's assumptions before using the results for financial planning.
