## What Does a Mutual Fund Distributor Do?

A mutual fund distributor is an intermediary who helps investors access mutual fund schemes and related services. A distributor may explain available processes, assist with transactions, and provide support within the scope of their registration and authorisation.

In India, investors may invest directly through a mutual fund or use a registered intermediary. These routes can differ in how services are provided and how costs or commissions work.

## How Can You Verify a Distributor?

Before relying on a distributor, check their registration information and credentials through official sources.

AMFI provides a distributor locator where users can search using available details, such as a distributor's name or ARN.

Official resource: https://www.amfiindia.com/locate-distributor

Do not rely only on a website's logo, social media page, or verbal claims. Check official registration information and ask questions about the service, costs, and possible conflicts of interest.

## Does a Mutual Fund Calculator Act as a Distributor?

No. A general-purpose calculator provides mathematical estimates. It does not become a mutual fund distributor merely by displaying investment projections.

A calculator should not make personalised investment recommendations unless the website is appropriately authorised to provide the relevant service.

## Direct Investing vs Using a Distributor

Investors should understand the relevant plan, service, costs, and official documentation before choosing how to invest. The appropriate route depends on their needs and understanding of the process.

A calculator alone cannot decide which route is right for an individual.

## Conclusion

A mutual fund distributor can provide assistance with investment-related processes, but investors should verify credentials and understand the services offered. Use official sources for registration checks and review fund documentation before making an investment decision.
